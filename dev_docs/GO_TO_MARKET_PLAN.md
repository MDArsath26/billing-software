# Go-to-Market Plan — Billing Software SaaS

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 11 November 2026

## 1. Launch objective
Validate a dependable cloud billing and inventory product with real retail shops before scaling acquisition or introducing mandatory subscriptions.

## 2. Initial target segment
Start with independent clothing retailers and small shops that need product/stock management, POS billing, customer records and basic reports. Prefer pilot shops where the owner can provide frequent workflow feedback.

## 3. Positioning
"A simple cloud billing and stock management system for retail shops, with assisted setup and a free core product at launch."

Avoid promising accounting, compliance or integrations that are not yet implemented and verified.

## 4. Launch offer
- Free core software at initial launch.
- Paid optional setup, product import, customization and training.
- No mandatory subscription at launch.
- Explain clearly what is included in free core and what is a separately quoted service.

## 5. Pilot programme
1. Recruit a small pilot group of shops.
2. Observe current billing and stock workflows.
3. Configure shop profile, categories and products.
4. Import data only after validating format and obtaining owner approval.
5. Train owner and staff.
6. Monitor first invoices, stock deduction and reports.
7. Collect weekly feedback and log defects.
8. Obtain explicit permission before using shop names, screenshots or testimonials publicly.

## 6. Onboarding journey
Lead → discovery call → workflow assessment → demo → scope/quote for paid services → shop setup → product import → staff training → first live invoice → follow-up and support.

## 7. Acquisition channels
- Direct visits and referrals from local retailers.
- Demonstrations through local business networks.
- Short product walkthrough videos.
- Website landing page and inquiry form.
- Retail/accounting consultants and local technology partners.

Measure leads and conversions by channel before increasing spend.

## 8. Sales/demo checklist
Demonstrate login, product creation, category/subcategory, stock, POS checkout, invoice printing, customer records, GST on/off behavior, dashboard and staff permissions. Use demo data, not another customer's real data.

## 9. Trust and support
- Publish clear setup/service scope and pricing.
- Provide a support contact and response expectations.
- Explain backups, data export and account cancellation/retention policies accurately.
- Do not claim certifications or compliance guarantees without evidence.

## 10. Metrics
Track leads, demos, pilot activation, time-to-first-invoice, weekly active shops, invoices per shop, stock discrepancy reports, support tickets, setup-service conversion, referral rate and retention.

## 11. Rollout gates
**Private pilot:** tenant isolation, authentication, invoice correctness, stock consistency, backups and basic support must pass.

**Public launch:** critical defects closed, onboarding documentation ready, privacy/terms reviewed, monitoring and incident process active.

**Subscription launch (later):** plan entitlements, trial, payment retries, grace, refunds, cancellation, renewal and reconciliation all tested.

## 12. Marketing content plan
Create concise material: product overview, 2-minute billing demo, stock-management walkthrough, onboarding guide, GST configuration explanation and pilot testimonials with written consent.
